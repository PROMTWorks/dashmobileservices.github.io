Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about DASH MOBILE SERVICES, our pre-launch phase, Interest Bookings, services, scheduling, and customer support.

What is DASH MOBILE SERVICES?

DASH MOBILE SERVICES is a multi-service company built around a simple idea: one company, multiple services, less hassle. We are building a convenient way for customers to access multiple types of services through one company.

What is an Interest Booking?

An Interest Booking lets you tell DASH that you are interested in a particular service. During our pre-launch phase, these requests help us understand what customers are looking for and prepare for our official launch.

Is an Interest Booking a confirmed appointment?

No. During the pre-launch phase, Interest Bookings are not guaranteed service appointments. They are a way for customers to express interest and help DASH understand what the community is looking for.

Why is DASH tracking customer traffic and booking activity?

During pre-launch, DASH is tracking customer traffic, service interest, and booking activity to better understand demand. This helps us prepare our services, scheduling, and operations around the needs of future customers.

When will DASH officially launch?

DASH is currently preparing for its official launch. Timing may depend on service readiness, scheduling, operations, and customer demand. Updates will be shared as we get closer to launch.

What areas will DASH serve?

DASH is currently focused on Myrtle Beach and surrounding areas. Service availability may vary depending on the service requested and our launch schedule.

Can I request a service that is not currently listed?

Yes. If there is a service you would like DASH to offer, you can let us know. Customer feedback and service interest can help us understand what additional services may be valuable to the community.

If you have a custom service request, you can contact DASH MOBILE SERVICES directly by phone or email and tell us where you need the service and when you would like it done. We will review your request and determine whether we can accommodate it.

How do I book a service?

Use Book a Service to review available service categories and submit a booking request. A booking is not officially confirmed until DASH completes its applicable confirmation process.

When is my service date confirmed?

DASH MOBILE SERVICES determines and confirms the applicable service date and location. Payment will only be requested once the service details, date, and location have been confirmed by DASH.

A payment request from DASH MOBILE SERVICES is part of the confirmed booking process. Payment must be sent and successfully processed before DASH can provide a confirmed service date.

If payment is not received within 5 days of the payment request, DASH MOBILE SERVICES will decline the booking. Until payment is received and processed, the service date should not be considered confirmed.

What happens if my payment is late?

If the required payment is not received by the applicable deadline, the booking may be placed into Payment Pending status. If the original service date passes before payment is received, the original date is no longer held and the customer may be placed on the DASH Wait List.

What Should I Include in My Payment?

When sending a payment through Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, or another payment method provided by DASH, customers should include their Job #/ID and the name used at the time of booking in the payment note or description. This information helps DASH correctly identify and apply the payment to the appropriate booking.

DASH MOBILE SERVICES is currently in the process of utilizing Square payment software to provide customers with an easier and more seamless payment experience. Additional payment instructions will be provided as the Square payment system becomes available.

What if I have a complaint about a completed service?

Customers may submit a complaint through the designated DASH complaint process. DASH may request photographs, videos, documentation, or other evidence and will review the available information before making a decision.

Can I receive a free re-service?

A complimentary re-service may be approved when DASH Admin determines that the evidence sufficiently establishes that the booked service was not properly completed. A customer preference or disagreement with DASH's established service method does not by itself qualify.

What if DASH cannot access my property?

The service address must be accessible when DASH personnel arrive. Customers are responsible for providing required gate codes, building information, unit numbers, parking instructions, and other access information during booking.

What if weather or an emergency affects my service?

DASH may delay, reschedule, modify, or discontinue a service when weather, emergencies, road conditions, utility issues, property emergencies, or other circumstances make service unsafe or impractical.

Can an employee approve a refund or change a policy?

DASH personnel may assist customers with service-related questions, but employees are not automatically authorized to make binding decisions about refunds, cancellations, discounts, complimentary services, or policy changes. Customers should rely on official DASH communications for administrative decisions.

How do I contact DASH MOBILE SERVICES?

Use the Contact DASH MOBILE SERVICES page for the current support phone number, temporary support email, service area, and contact-hour information.
